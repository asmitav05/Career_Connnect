UNDERSTAND THE CAREER

What does a Civil Services Officer do?

Civil servants work in different areas of government administration. Their responsibilities can include implementing government policies, managing public programmes, administration and working with communities.

The civil services include several services and roles. The exact work depends on the service, cadre and position.

💡 Good fit if you enjoy...
  • Understanding society
  • Problem solving
  • Reading and analysis
  • Leadership and responsibility

THINK DIFFERENTLY

Common misconceptions

Don't let common assumptions stop you from exploring a career that may suit you.

MYTH

“You must study Arts.”

REALITY

Students from different academic backgrounds can explore Civil Services. Your graduation subject does not have to be Arts.

MYTH

“Only Political Science students can qualify.”

REALITY

Civil Services preparation covers a broad range of subjects, so students come from many different educational backgrounds.

MYTH

“There is only one path into public service.”

REALITY

Besides UPSC, students can also explore State Public Service Commission examinations and other public-sector career routes.

MYTH

“The exam is the whole career.”

REALITY

The examination is the selection process. The actual career involves administration, governance, responsibility and public service.
