ENTRANCE EXAMS

Which entrance route should you explore?

The entrance examination depends on the programme and institution you choose.

🔬
RESEARCH-FOCUSED SCIENCE

IISER Aptitude Test (IAT)

Used for admission to IISER programmes, including BS-MS and other eligible programmes offered through the IISER admission process.

🎓
UNDERGRADUATE ADMISSIONS

CUET-UG

Used for undergraduate admissions at participating Central Universities and other participating universities. The available programmes depend on each institution.

🧪
INTEGRATED SCIENCE

NEST

Entrance route for the five-year Integrated MSc programme at NISER Bhubaneswar and UM-DAE CEBS Mumbai.

🏛️
INSTITUTION-SPECIFIC

JEE (Advanced) / Other Routes

Some research-oriented science programmes may have additional admission routes. For example, IISc's BS (Research) programme has included JEE (Advanced) and IAT routes.

💡 Important

Entrance exams, eligibility and participating institutions can change by academic year. Always verify the latest admission information on the official institution or examination website.

SKILLS TO BUILD

What skills should you develop?

Scientific careers require curiosity, analytical thinking, strong subject knowledge and the ability to investigate questions systematically.

🔬 Scientific Thinking

Learn to ask questions, form hypotheses, analyse evidence and draw conclusions using scientific reasoning.

🧠 Analytical Thinking

Develop the ability to understand complex problems, identify patterns and interpret information logically.

📊 Data Analysis

Learn to organise, analyse and interpret scientific data using appropriate mathematical and computational methods.

🧪 Experimentation

Understand experimental methods, careful observation, measurement and responsible handling of scientific results.

💻 Programming & Computing

Learn computational tools and programming where they are useful for data analysis, modelling, simulations or research.

📚 Research & Communication

Learn to read scientific literature, document your work and communicate findings clearly through reports and presentations.

CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

Where can science & research take you?

Science offers career opportunities across research, laboratories, technology, education and specialised scientific fields.

🔬 Research Scientist

Conduct experiments, analyse evidence and contribute to new scientific knowledge in research institutions, universities or industry.

🧪 Laboratory Scientist

Work with scientific equipment, experiments, testing and laboratory procedures in specialised fields.

🌌 Physicist

Study matter, energy, forces and physical systems through theoretical, experimental or applied research.

🧬 Biologist

Study living organisms and biological processes through research, laboratory work and field studies.

⚗️ Chemist

Study substances, chemical processes and materials through experimentation and scientific analysis.

📊 Scientific Data Analyst

Use mathematics, statistics and computational tools to analyse scientific datasets and support research.

COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS

Let's clear up a few myths about science & research

Science careers are diverse, and many common assumptions do not reflect the reality of working in this field.

MYTH

“You can only become a scientist after a PhD.”

THE TRUTH

A PhD is important for many independent research careers, but science graduates can also explore laboratory, education, technical and industry-related roles.

MYTH

“Science careers are only about working in laboratories.”

THE TRUTH

Science careers can involve fieldwork, computing, data analysis, teaching, scientific communication, industry and many other areas.

MYTH

“You need to be good at every science subject.”

THE TRUTH

Different scientific fields require different subject strengths. You can specialise in an area that matches your interests and abilities.

MYTH

“Research means doing the same experiment every day.”

THE TRUTH

Research can involve experiments, literature review, data analysis, modelling, programming, collaboration and communicating scientific findings.

COMMON MISCONCEPTIONS

Let's clear up a few myths

Science and research careers are broader than many students initially think. Here are some common misconceptions to keep in mind.

MYTH

"Science careers are only for people who work in laboratories."

THE TRUTH

Science careers can include research, data analysis, fieldwork, education, technology and many specialised areas.

MYTH

"You must become a doctor to have a career in science."

THE TRUTH

Science offers many paths including physics, chemistry, biology, mathematics, research and other scientific disciplines.

MYTH

"Research means only doing experiments all day."

THE TRUTH

Research can also involve reading literature, analysing data, programming, modelling, writing and communicating findings.

MYTH

"A science degree limits you to one type of job."

THE TRUTH

Science graduates can build careers across research, education, laboratories, technology, data-related roles and industry.
