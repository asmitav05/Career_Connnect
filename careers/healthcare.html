🎓 EDUCATION

Choose a Relevant Course

Options include MBBS, BDS, BAMS, BHMS, B.Pharm, B.Sc. Nursing, physiotherapy and other healthcare-related programs.

📝 ENTRANCE

Depends on the Profession

For example, NEET-UG is used for admission to MBBS, BDS and several other medical and allied health programs. Other courses may have different admission routes.

🧠 CORE SKILLS

Knowledge & Human Skills

Develop scientific knowledge, communication, observation, critical thinking, empathy and responsible decision-making.

💼 OPPORTUNITIES

Many Healthcare Roles

Career options include doctors, dentists, pharmacists, nurses, physiotherapists, psychologists, researchers and other healthcare professionals.

ENTRANCE EXAMS

Which entrance exam do you need?

The entrance route depends on the healthcare course you choose. Always check the latest admission rules before applying.

🩺
MEDICAL & DENTAL

NEET-UG

Used for undergraduate medical education including MBBS and BDS, and also for several AYUSH undergraduate courses such as BAMS, BUMS, BSMS and BHMS.

💊
PHARMACY

MHT-CET

In Maharashtra, MHT-CET is an entrance route for undergraduate Pharmacy and Pharm.D. admissions under the State CET Cell process.

👩‍⚕️
NURSING

MH-Nursing-CET

Maharashtra conducts MH-Nursing-CET for admission to B.Sc. Nursing and General Nursing & Midwifery (GNM) courses through the State CET Cell.

🎓
OTHER HEALTHCARE COURSES

Course-Specific Admission

Physiotherapy, psychology and other healthcare-related programs may follow different entrance or admission processes depending on the institution and state.

💡 Important

Entrance requirements can change by course, institution and academic year. Check the official examination or admission authority before applying.

SKILLS TO BUILD

What should you develop?

Healthcare requires both professional knowledge and strong interpersonal skills.

🧬 Scientific Knowledge

Build a strong foundation in biology and other subjects relevant to your chosen healthcare field.

🧠 Critical Thinking

Learn to analyse information carefully and make responsible, evidence-based decisions.

🗣️ Communication

Communicate clearly and respectfully with patients, families and professional teams.

❤️ Empathy

Develop patience, compassion and respect when working with people.

🔬 Research Skills

Learn how to understand scientific information and explore developments in healthcare.

🤝 Teamwork

Healthcare professionals often work as part of multidisciplinary teams.

CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

Where can healthcare take you?

Healthcare offers several professional paths depending on your education and interests.

🩺 Doctor

Diagnose and manage medical conditions and provide patient care after completing the required medical education and training.

🦷 Dentist

Provide oral healthcare, diagnosis and dental treatment.

💊 Pharmacist

Work with medicines, their safe use, dispensing and pharmaceutical services.

👩‍⚕️ Nurse

Provide patient care, support treatment and work with healthcare teams.

🧠 Psychologist

Study behaviour and mental processes and work in areas such as counselling, assessment, research or applied psychology depending on qualifications.

🔬 Healthcare Researcher

Conduct research that contributes to better understanding, prevention and treatment of health-related problems.
