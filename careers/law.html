LAW ENTRANCE EXAMS

Which entrance exams can you take?

The entrance exam depends on the law programme and university you want to join.

⚖️

CLAT

5-Year Integrated Law

Common Law Admission Test used for admission to participating National Law Universities and other institutions.

🏛️

AILET

5-Year Integrated Law

All India Law Entrance Test for admission to undergraduate law programmes at National Law University Delhi.

🎓

MAH-LL.B. 5 Yrs CET

After 12th

Maharashtra's entrance examination for admission to five-year integrated LL.B. programmes through the state admission process.

📚

MAH-LL.B. 3 Yrs CET

After Graduation

Maharashtra's entrance examination for admission to three-year LL.B. programmes.

🎯

University-Specific Exams

Depends on Institution

Some universities and institutions may have their own entrance examination or admission process. Always check the institution's official website.

💡 Important

Entrance requirements, participating institutions and admission rules can change. Always verify the latest eligibility and admission information on the official examination or university website.

UNDERSTAND THE CAREER

What does a legal professional do?

Legal professionals work with laws, legal documents and disputes. Depending on their role, they may represent clients, provide legal advice, conduct research or support organisations with legal matters.

The legal field includes many different career paths. Your responsibilities depend on your specialisation, workplace and role.

💡 Good fit if you enjoy...
  • Reading and research
  • Logical reasoning
  • Debate and communication
  • Understanding rules and society
