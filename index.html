Frequently Asked Questions

Real answers to help students and parents navigate career choices with clarity.

View All FAQs →
Which stream should I choose after Class 10? +
Choose Science (PCM) for engineering, defense, and architecture; Science (PCB) for medicine, pharmacy, and bio-sciences; Commerce for finance, CA, and corporate business; Arts/Humanities for law, UPSC civil services, journalism, and design.
Can I get a software or tech job without an engineering degree? +
Yes! Degrees like BCA (Bachelor of Computer Applications), B.Sc. Computer Science, or self-taught web and mobile development portfolios are widely recognized by tech companies and product startups across India.
How do I apply for government scholarships? +
Applications are processed via the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) or State portals like MahaDBT. You will need an active Aadhaar-seeded bank account, family income certificate from the Tehsildar, and college admission fee receipts.
