7. Mandatory Verification Documents Checklist

Ensure you maintain original and scanned copies (PDF format under 200KB) of these statutory documents before counseling and scholarship portals commence:

  • 1.
    Annual Family Income Certificate (Tahsil / SDO Officer):

    Must be issued for the current financial year by a designated executive magistrate. Private employer pay slips are not accepted by government portals.

  • 2.
    Aadhaar Card with Bank Account NPCI Seeding:

    Your active savings account must be mapped to NPCI (National Payments Corporation of India) for Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) scholarship payments.

  • 3.
    Domicile Certificate & Nationality Proof:

    Certifies residency in your home state to avail state reservation quotas and state fee concession schemes.

  • 4.
    Caste Certificate & Caste Validity Certificate:

    Required for SC, ST, OBC, VJNT reservation seats. In states like Maharashtra, Caste Validity is mandatory for professional college admissions.
